01 — Overview

What you’re
looking at.

This is a working draft. The design, layout, features and page structure are all built and working. Several details are still waiting on information from you.

A premium, architectural website built around the Botwin BR mark: bold black geometry, clean off-white space, and a clear story of transformation.

The site opens with a full-screen scene that transforms as visitors scroll: raw framing → construction → finished space. From there it walks through before & after comparisons, services, selected projects, your process and a simple consultation request form.

Three things to know as you review

  • 01
    The images are placeholdersThe black-and-white room drawings are illustrations and are labelled that way on the site. They will be replaced with photos of your real projects.
  • 02
    “To be confirmed” = we need your infoWe never publish unverified numbers, licenses or reviews. Anything grey or dashed is waiting on you (see the checklist at the end).
  • 03
    Your logo is built inThe BR mark has been redrawn as sharp vector artwork from your logo file, so it stays crisp at every size, from the phone tab icon to the footer.
